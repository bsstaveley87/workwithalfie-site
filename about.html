About

Most CRM rules live in someone's head

{{ site.entity.definition }}

Ask a growing sales team who owns Acme and you'll get two answers. Ask which of the two Acme Inc records is the real one and you'll get a shrug. The rules exist. Nobody wrote them down, so they get settled one argument at a time.

Why we built Alfie

We built Alfie after watching a fast-growing SaaS team's HubSpot setup fall behind the business. Who owned which account, and what a deal needed before it could move to the next stage, lived in one person's head. When that person was busy, nobody knew. We wrote the rules down one problem at a time, then built software to check changes against them before they reached HubSpot. Alfie is that approach, offered to other growing teams.

What we believe

Your team shouldn't spend its evenings on CRM chores

Merging records and chasing missing fields is necessary work, and it's exactly the kind a machine should do.

Write the rule down before you automate it

Automating a process nobody agreed on just makes the mess faster.

Automation needs governance to be worth trusting

Alfie works from your written rules, checks before it writes, and leaves the calls that are hard to undo, like merges, to a named person.

The rules are yours

You get them in writing, and you keep them if you leave.

Start small

Clear the duplicates and the missing close dates before redesigning the whole funnel.
