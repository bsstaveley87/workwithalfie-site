About

Most CRM rules live in someone's head.

Who owns which account. What a deal needs before it moves. Which duplicate wins. In most growing SaaS companies, those rules are unwritten until something breaks. Alfie exists to get them written down and running, starting with the basics.

Why we built Alfie.

We built Alfie after watching a fast-growing SaaS team's HubSpot setup fall behind the business. The rules for who owned which account, and what a deal needed before it moved, lived in one person's head. We wrote them down one problem at a time, then built software to check every change against them before it reached HubSpot. Alfie brings that approach to other growing teams: written rules you own, checked on every change, with a person signing off where it matters.

What we believe.

Rules before automation

Alfie only runs rules you've approved. Automating a process nobody agreed on just makes the mess faster.

People decide the important things

Alfie proposes. Rules check. A person approves where it matters.

You own your rules

The document is yours, whether or not you keep using Alfie.

Basics first, one process at a time

A small change that works beats a big plan that doesn't ship.
