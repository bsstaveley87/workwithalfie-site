Security

You decide what Alfie can touch

You're handing Alfie real work inside your HubSpot: merges, field fixes, routing. So you should know what it can access, how it makes changes, and where your data goes. This page describes how Alfie is designed. If you need it in questionnaire form, ask us.

{% include cta.html %}How it works

HubSpot permissions, one process at a time

Alfie asks only for the HubSpot permissions the processes you use need, and your admin installs it. When you add a process that needs a new permission, Alfie asks for it then, and your admin decides then. Nothing is requested up front "just in case".

A record of each change Alfie makes

AI can draft a change. Fixed checks written from your rules decide whether it's allowed. Alfie is designed to keep a record of each change it makes that can't be edited afterward. The record holds the request, what was proposed, which rule checked it (and which version of that rule), who approved it, the value before and after, and the read-back from HubSpot.

How you see it

  • A summary in Slack
  • A downloadable list of exceptions
  • A full export whenever you ask, including when you leave

What Alfie does on its own, and what waits for a person

On its own, Alfie checks records, drafts fixes and prepares changes. Whether a change is written straight away is up to your rules. At the start, every change waits for a yes in Slack. A rule runs on its own only after your admin signs it off, and that sign-off is recorded against that version of the rule. Merges always wait for your admin, and so does anything else your rules reserve for a named person.

How Alfie confirms a change

After each write, Alfie reads the record back from HubSpot by its ID. The change isn't marked done until the values match. If they don't, Alfie flags the mismatch.

Where AI is used, and where it stops

Alfie uses AI in two places from the start: to read requests and draft changes, and to suggest which companies are parents and subsidiaries during cleanup. Every AI suggestion goes through the same rule checks as any other change. Nothing is linked or changed in HubSpot on an AI suggestion alone. We send the model only the fields a task needs, on terms where it isn't used to train models. How long AI-processed data is kept is set per customer, in your configuration.

ForecastAI (if added): reads the activity logged on your deals in HubSpot, including transcripts where available, and never changes anything. See ForecastAI.

Where your data lives

Isolated per customer

Alfie is hosted on established cloud infrastructure. Each customer's data is kept in its own database.

Encrypted

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The keys that connect Alfie to your HubSpot and Slack are encrypted with a key unique to your account.

When you leave

We export your data and your change record for you, then delete it. Your rules document is yours either way.

We're new, and we don't hold security certifications yet. If your team has a security questionnaire, send it over or bring it to a call and we'll go through it with you.
