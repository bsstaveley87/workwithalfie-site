Security

You decide what Alfie can touch.

Alfie works inside your HubSpot, so you should know exactly what it can access, how it changes things, and where your data goes. Here's how it's designed.

{% include cta.html %}How it works

Only the permissions each process needs.

Alfie asks only for the HubSpot permissions each process needs, and adds more only with your admin's approval. When a new process ships, any new permission it needs is requested then, not up front. Your admin approves the install.

Every change checked. Every change recorded.

AI can propose a change, but fixed checks written from your approved rules decide whether it's allowed. Alfie is designed to keep a record of every change that can't be edited after the fact: the request, what was proposed, which rule checked it and which version of the rule, who approved it, the value before and after, and the read-back from HubSpot. You can see and export a record of every change.

How you see it

  • A summary in Slack
  • A list of exceptions you can download
  • A full export whenever you ask, and when you leave

A person decides where your rules say so.

Your rules say which changes need a person, and which person. Alfie asks in Slack and waits. At the start, every change waits for approval. A rule runs on its own only after your admin signs it off, and that sign-off is recorded against that version of the rule. Merging records always needs a fresh approval from your admin.

Done means confirmed.

After each write, Alfie reads the record back from HubSpot by ID. Nothing counts as done until the value matches. If it doesn't, Alfie flags it instead of hiding it.

AI suggests. Your rules decide.

Alfie uses AI from the start. In data cleanup, Alfie uses AI to suggest parent and subsidiary company links, and nothing changes in HubSpot until you approve it. It also reads requests and proposes changes. Every AI suggestion goes through the same rule checks as any other change, and a person approves where your rules say so. We send the model only the fields a task needs, on terms where it isn't used to train models. How long any AI-processed data is kept is set per customer, in your configuration.

ForecastAI (if added): reads the activity logged on your deals in HubSpot, including transcripts where available, and never changes anything. See ForecastAI.

Kept separate, encrypted, and yours.

Isolated per customer

Hosted on established cloud infrastructure, isolated per customer. Each customer's data is kept in its own database.

Encrypted

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The keys that connect Alfie to your HubSpot and Slack are encrypted with a key unique to your account.

Yours when you leave

If you stop using Alfie, we export your data and your change record for you, then delete it. Your rules document is yours either way.

We're new, and we don't hold security certifications yet. If your team needs a security questionnaire answered, ask us on a call and we'll go through it with you.
