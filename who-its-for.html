Who it's for

Who runs your HubSpot today?

In a growing SaaS company, it's usually one of three people, and none of them were hired to do it. Alfie works with all three. You don't need to know the word RevOps. (It means the rules and upkeep that keep your CRM, pipeline and account ownership straight.)

{% include cta.html %}

The founder

You shouldn't still be your company's HubSpot admin

You set up HubSpot when there were two of you. Now there are 25 people, and you're still the one who knows why the "Lifecycle Stage (old)" property exists. Alfie takes the upkeep: the merges, the missing fields, the new contacts nobody assigned. You agree a short set of standard rules once, and after that Alfie only comes to you for the calls your rules reserve for you.

The person who inherited it

You picked it up along the way

You run marketing, or sales ops, and HubSpot came with the job. The rules exist, but only in your head: enterprise deals go to Dana, partner leads go to whoever's free. We write them down with you, and Alfie does the upkeep the same way every week, including the weeks you're on vacation.

An early RevOps hire

You're the first RevOps hire

You know what's wrong with the CRM. You've had a list since your first week. You just can't get to it between board prep and the Monday pipeline review. Think of Alfie as a second pair of hands: you set the rules, and Alfie does the merging, reassigning and field-fixing, each change checked and recorded. You get your week back for the next process.

How it works

If the job is mostly upkeep

Most HubSpot upkeep repeats: tidying after an import, chasing deals with no close date, moving a leaver's accounts. You could hire for that or buy a freelance admin's hours. Alfie takes on the repeat work itself, by rules you set, and leaves the judgment calls and bigger projects to your team and any experts you already use.

Probably a good fit if

  • You're a growing SaaS team and HubSpot is your CRM
  • Your team works in Slack (Alfie needs it for requests and approvals)
  • You don't have a RevOps team yet, or you have one person who's stretched thin

Probably not a fit if

  • Salesforce is your CRM. Alfie only works with HubSpot.
  • You already have a RevOps team, with your rules written down and running.
  • You want a one-off cleanup and nothing after. Alfie is for keeping the setup in step as you grow.
