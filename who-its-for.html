Who it's for

Who runs your HubSpot today?

At a growing SaaS team, HubSpot is usually run by one of three people. Alfie works with all three. You don't need the RevOps vocabulary (RevOps: the rules and upkeep that keep your CRM, pipeline and ownership straight). You need someone who's done it before to tell you what to fix first.

{% include cta.html %}

The founder

You shouldn't still be your company's HubSpot admin.

You've run HubSpot since the first deal, and it's still on your desk. Alfie takes it off your plate without a full-time hire, and you keep sign-off on what matters.

What changes: You approve a short set of standard rules once. After that, Alfie checks your HubSpot against them and asks you only when a rule says a person needs to decide.

The person who inherited it

You picked it up along the way.

A sales, marketing or ops lead who picked up HubSpot along the way. Alfie gives you written rules and does the upkeep, without a full-time hire.

What changes: The rules stop living in your head. They're written down, you own them, and Alfie applies them the same way every time.

An early RevOps hire

A second pair of hands.

Alfie is your second pair of hands. You set the rules and Alfie runs them, instead of you making a second hire.

What changes: You spend your time on the next process, not on merging duplicates and fixing owners. Every change Alfie makes is checked against your rules and recorded.

A good fit if

  • A growing SaaS team using HubSpot as its CRM
  • Slack is where your team works (Alfie needs it for requests and approvals)
  • No full RevOps team yet, or one person who needs help

Probably not a fit if

  • Salesforce is your CRM. Alfie works with HubSpot only.
  • You already have a RevOps team with your rules written down and running.
  • You want a one-off cleanup and nothing after it. Alfie keeps the setup in step as you grow.
