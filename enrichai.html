Add-on

The right accounts, and the people in them, in HubSpot.

EnrichAI is the optional add-on for all paid data work. It finds contacts, emails and phone numbers at your target accounts, adds new companies that fit your ICP, and keeps your ad audience lists up to date in HubSpot. Everything it writes goes through the same rule checks and approvals as the rest of Alfie.

{% include cta.html %}See core features

Four jobs, one set of rules.

Contacts at your target accounts

Finds the people who match your buying roles at the accounts you're going after, then adds them to HubSpot with a work email and phone number where one can be found.

New accounts that fit your ICP

Finds companies that match your ideal customer profile and adds them to your target account list, so the list grows without anyone researching by hand.

Audiences for ads

Builds and maintains audience lists of contacts at your target accounts in HubSpot, with hashed (pseudonymized) emails for ad matching, ready for HubSpot's ads integration to sync to your ad platforms.

Company and contact details

Fills in firmographics and professional details on records you already have, one at a time or as a whole list.

EnrichAI is an add-on for Alfie customers. Any data bought or sourced from outside your CRM is EnrichAI.

Sourced, checked, then written.

Every value has a source

Each new value records where it came from and when it was found.

Your rules set the bar

You decide which sources count for each field and how sure Alfie must be before it writes.

Your team's entries stay put

Alfie won't overwrite a value a person entered unless your rules allow it or someone approves.

When sources disagree, nothing changes

The existing value is kept, and the record is flagged for review.

How every change is checked

The best source for each record, not one fixed vendor.

EnrichAI checks several paid data providers in turn and uses the most cost-effective one that meets your rules for each field. If the first source can't find a value or doesn't clear your bar, it tries the next.
