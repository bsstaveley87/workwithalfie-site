Add-on

Complete records, without the research

EnrichAI fills in your company and contact records so nobody has to look things up or type them in. It finds the people in your buying roles at your target accounts, adds the missing company and contact details, brings in new companies that fit your ICP, and keeps ad audience lists current in HubSpot. With the fields filled in, routing and segments have something to work with.

What your team stops doing by hand

People at your target accounts

Say Acme is on your list and all you have there is a billing contact. EnrichAI finds the people in your buying roles and adds them to HubSpot, with a work email and phone number where one can be found. Reps start with names, not a research task.

Missing details on the records you have

Firmographics like industry and headcount, plus professional details like job title, for one record or a whole list. With those fields filled in, new contacts and companies route to the right owner, you can build segments by industry or size, and fewer leads land with the wrong person.

New accounts that fit your ICP

EnrichAI finds companies that match your ideal customer profile and adds them to your target account list. Each new company goes through the same parent and subsidiary suggestions as the rest of Alfie, like Acme EU under Acme, and a person approves each link before anything changes in HubSpot.

Ad audiences that keep up

EnrichAI builds and maintains HubSpot lists of contacts at your target accounts, with hashed (pseudonymized) emails for ad matching, ready for HubSpot's ads integration to sync. Your ads reach the accounts sales is working, and nobody exports a CSV to get them there.

EnrichAI is for Alfie customers. Any data bought or sourced from outside your CRM counts as EnrichAI.

You stay in control

Your team's data comes first

A phone number a rep typed in stays put unless your rules allow an overwrite or someone OKs it. When sources disagree, the existing value wins and the record is flagged for review. Each new value records where it came from and when it was found, so anyone can see why a field says what it says. You choose which sources count for each field, and how sure Alfie has to be before it writes.

How every change is checked

Fewer blank fields

EnrichAI checks several paid data providers in turn. If the first can't find a value, or the value doesn't meet your bar, it tries the next, so fewer fields are left empty. For each field, it uses the most cost-effective source that meets your rules. How long enrichment data is kept is agreed with each customer at setup.
