Privacy notice

This covers workwithalfie.com and its contact form. Last updated October 22, 2026.

Who we are

Alfie is a US company. If you have a question about your details, email ben@workwithalfie.com.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your work email
  • your company
  • your message

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers may log basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell your details.

Who handles it

A small number of service providers help us run the site and deliver your message to us. Cloudflare processes the form submission and runs its spam check, and your submission reaches us by email. They handle your details only to provide those services.

Spam protection

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is sending it. The check runs in your browser when you reach the form.

Cookies

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to talk with you about it. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, email ben@workwithalfie.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top.
