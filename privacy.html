Privacy notice

This covers the contact form on workwithalfie.com. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your work email
  • your company
  • your message

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry.

Where it goes

Your submission is emailed to Alfie. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Spam protection

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is sending it. Turnstile runs a short check in your browser when the page loads.

Deleting your details

To ask us to delete what you sent, email ben@workwithalfie.com.
