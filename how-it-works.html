How it works

One change, from request to verified.

Every change Alfie makes follows the same four steps. Here's what happens at each one, and how the first weeks go after you connect HubSpot.

{% include cta.html %}See core features

Request. Rule check. Approval if needed. Verified.

  1. 01

    Request

    A change starts in one of two ways: someone asks in Slack ("Move Acme to Sarah"), one record or a pasted list, or Alfie spots something your rules cover, like a deal at Proposal with no close date. Alfie uses AI to read the request and your HubSpot data, and proposes one specific change.

    ExampleRequest from Jordan: assign Acme Ltd to Sarah.

  2. 02

    Rule check

    Fixed checks, written from your approved rules, decide whether the change is allowed. AI proposes; it doesn't get the final say. If the change fails a rule, nothing is written and Alfie says which rule it failed.

    ExampleTerritory passes. Over owner cap (41 of 40).

  3. 03

    Approval if needed

    Your rules say which changes need a person, and which person. Alfie asks them in Slack, with the reason, and waits. Nothing happens until they approve.

    ExampleRule 1.1: going over cap needs the sales lead. @Dana [Approve] [Decline]

  4. 04

    Verified in HubSpot

    Alfie writes the change, then reads the record back by ID. Nothing counts as done until it matches. If it doesn't match, Alfie flags it rather than hiding it. Every step goes into a record you can see and export.

    ExampleApproved by Dana. Acme assigned to Sarah. Confirmed in HubSpot.

Verified means the value was confirmed right after the write. If someone changes it later, that's a new change, and Alfie flags it if it breaks a rule.

The rollout

Alfie earns trust one rule at a time.

  1. 01

    Week one: read-only

    Alfie connects to HubSpot and reads. It runs your approved rules in the background without changing anything, and gives you a baseline: where your data stands against the rules today.

  2. 02

    Next: every change approved

    Alfie starts proposing fixes. Every write waits for a person to approve it in Slack.

  3. 03

    Then: rules you trust run on their own

    When your admin is happy with a rule, they sign it off, and that rule can run without asking. Sign-off is recorded against that version of the rule. If the rule changes, it's signed off again.

Always approved. Merging records always needs a fresh approval from your admin, however long you've used Alfie.

Your rules, in writing, yours to keep.

The standard rules you approve are written down as your rules of engagement: who owns what, which fields are required, who approves exceptions. Alfie keeps your approved rules and gives you a written copy, your rules of engagement, that you can keep wherever you like, such as Notion or Google Docs. To change a rule, tell us; we update it and send you the new version. Your copy comes as Markdown or PDF. It's yours whether or not you keep using Alfie, and it's updated as each new process ships.

See an example rules document

Clean data first. Ownership next.

We start where most HubSpot setups need it most. Month one covers clean data and required fields: duplicates found by rule and merged once your admin approves, fields standardized, and deals flagged when they reach a stage without the details your team agreed. Alfie also uses AI to suggest parent and subsidiary company links, and nothing changes in HubSpot until you approve it. Routing and account ownership usually follow in month two. After that, add the next process when you're ready.

At the end of month one, you'll have:

  • A written, approved set of rules for clean data and required fields
  • A baseline of where your data stood, and what's been fixed since
  • Any rules your admin has signed off running on their own, and everything else waiting for approval
