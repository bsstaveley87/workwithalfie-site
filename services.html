For Alfie customers

For the processes nobody has written down

Alfie runs on standard rules, and most of the basics fit them. Some processes don't. Maybe leads from partners follow a handoff that only your head of sales understands. Maybe nobody has ever decided what happens when a customer expands into a new region. For those, we start by finding out how the process actually works today. Then we design the fix with you, write it down, and Alfie takes the repetitive part over.

Three ways we can help

For Alfie customers who want more hands-on help.

Rules-of-engagement sprint

We sit down with your team and write the rules out: who owns what, who approves what, what each stage needs. You end up with something Alfie can run.

Custom builds

Fixed-fee projects for processes and integrations beyond the standard set.

Monthly RevOps hours

A set block of RevOps time each month for questions, reports and fixes.

You don't need to know the jargon

Most people who call us don't say "RevOps". They say "our reps keep fighting over accounts" or "I don't trust the pipeline number". That's enough. You need someone who has done this before to tell you what to fix first.
