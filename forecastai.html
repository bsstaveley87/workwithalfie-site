Add-on

A forecast that doesn't wait for rep updates.

Optional add-on: a weekly leadership forecast built from the activity in HubSpot, read-only, never shown to reps. It reads the emails, meeting notes and call notes logged on your deals, and transcripts where available, and gives you a second view of the pipeline. Your pipeline, fields and process stay exactly as they are.

A second view of every deal, built from what's logged.

Built from the activity logged in HubSpot

Emails, meeting notes and call notes logged on each deal, plus transcripts where available.

A view of each deal

For each deal with enough activity, ForecastAI gives its own view of value, stage, likelihood and next step, and points to the activity it's based on. Where a deal has too little activity to go on, it says "insufficient evidence" rather than guess.

Runs on its own

Fully automated. No one updates, approves or prompts anything, and nothing changes for your reps. The forecast arrives in Slack for your leadership team, weekly by default, on your schedule. The leaders you name can also ask for it any time.

Shows where the activity and the pipeline differ

Alfie's forecast next to what reps entered and HubSpot's weighted pipeline, by period, plus deals at risk and deals that slipped or moved forward.

ExampleAcme Ltd · Pipeline: this quarter, $40k · ForecastAI: at risk, $32k · Based on activity logged Oct 14

Nothing in your pipeline changes.

ForecastAI is read-only. It never edits deals, fields or stages, and it adds nothing to your reps' day: no new fields, no prompts, no approvals. The forecast goes only to the leadership team members you name, and it sits alongside your pipeline, not inside it.

Your forecast arrives as a Slack digest for the leadership team members you name, weekly by default, on your schedule.

Read-only, and only for the people you name.

ForecastAI reads the activity your team already logs in HubSpot: emails, meeting notes, call notes, and transcripts where available. It never changes anything in HubSpot. It's designed to keep only the short excerpts and summary signals it needs. How long any AI-processed data is kept is set per customer, in your configuration. Forecasts go only to the leadership team members you name. Your data isn't used to train AI models.

Easy to check against what actually closed.

Judge ForecastAI the way you'd judge any forecast: compare the forecast at the start of the quarter with the revenue actually closed by quarter end. Each digest shows ForecastAI's view next to what reps entered and HubSpot's weighted pipeline, so you can see over time how much weight to give it.

ForecastAI is an add-on for Alfie customers, ready to add to your plan now. It connects to HubSpot with its own read-only permissions, approved by your admin, and uses the same Slack setup as core Alfie. Setup takes a few weeks after kickoff, while we connect it to your pipeline and agree who gets the digest and when.
