Add-on

A forecast that runs itself

ForecastAI builds your leadership forecast from what's actually in HubSpot, deal by deal, and posts it to Slack weekly by default, on the day and time you choose. It's fully automated. Nobody spends Friday chasing reps to update their deals, and nobody rebuilds the pipeline-review spreadsheet. Deals at risk are called out, with the activity behind each call.

What lands in Slack

A view of every deal, with the evidence

For each deal, ForecastAI gives its view of value, stage, likelihood and next step, built from the emails, meeting notes and call notes logged on it, plus transcripts where available. Each view links to the activity it's based on. Deals with too little recent activity are listed separately as low coverage instead of being guessed.

Its number next to yours

ForecastAI's forecast sits beside what reps entered and HubSpot's weighted pipeline, by period. You see the gap before the meeting, not during it.

What moved since last time

Deals that slipped and deals that moved forward. Flags deals at risk, including ones that have gone quiet, and says why.

On your schedule

Weekly by default, on the day and time you set. Named leaders on the digest list can ask for an up-to-date version in Slack whenever they need one. Reps can't request it, and it's never sent to them.

ExampleAcme · Pipeline: this quarter, $40k · ForecastAI: at risk, $32k · Based on activity logged Oct 14

See why the number moved

Every forecast records the method and the activity it used, so you can see why a number moved from one week to the next. It's built from the activity logged in HubSpot and each deal's stage and fields, not from whoever updated their deals on Thursday. Your team keeps working the way it does now: same stages, same fields, no new tool to learn.

Check it against what closes

Each digest shows ForecastAI's view next to the reps' numbers and HubSpot's weighted pipeline. Note what it said at the start of the quarter and compare it with what actually closed. After a quarter or two, you'll know how much weight to give it.

What it reads, and who sees it

Optional add-on: a weekly leadership forecast built from the activity logged in HubSpot, read-only, never shown to reps. It uses its own read-only permissions, set up with your admin, and changes nothing in HubSpot. It's designed to keep only the short excerpts and summary signals it needs. How long AI-processed data is kept is set per customer, in your configuration, and your data isn't used to train AI models.

How we handle access and data

ForecastAI is ready to add to your plan now. It uses the same Slack setup as Alfie. Setup takes a few weeks after kickoff, while we connect it to your pipeline and agree who gets the forecast and when.
