For SaaS teams running on HubSpot

Your HubSpot was set up for a team half this size.

HubSpot is fine. The setup never caught up, so the cleanup lands on whoever has a spare evening, usually the founder, usually on a Sunday: merging the two "Acme Inc" records, chasing deals at Proposal with no close date, moving 300 accounts off the rep who left in March. Alfie takes that work on. It finds the problems, prepares the fixes against your rules, and makes the changes once your admin gives the go-ahead.

You stay in control

Why it's safe to hand this over

Automation with governance, in practice: Alfie works from rules you write down with us. AI drafts each change, then fixed checks decide whether it's allowed. Merges and anything else your rules reserve for a person wait for that person in Slack. After a write, Alfie reads the record back from HubSpot to confirm it stuck, and each change goes into a record you can export.

How we start

No written rules? That's normal.

Most teams we talk to have never written their CRM rules down. We start from a standard set and adjust it on a call with you.

  1. 01

    Quick start

    We connect HubSpot and Slack and agree the first rules with you: clean data, required fields, and who new contacts and companies go to.

  2. 02

    Day one

    Alfie checks your records and proposes fixes, and starts working through the backlog as your admin OKs them. Rules your admin trusts can then run on their own.

  3. 03

    The next process

    When you're ready, we pick what's next, agree what "done" looks like, and Alfie takes it on.

  4. 04

    The messy ones

    Custom processes, or ones nobody ever defined, go to Services.

How it works, step by step

Yours to keep

A rules document you own

The rules we agree become version one of your rules of engagement: who owns which accounts, what a deal needs at each stage, how duplicates are matched. Alfie keeps them and works from them. You get a written copy as Markdown or PDF, for Notion, Google Docs or wherever your team looks things up. To change a rule, tell us, and we'll send the new version. It's yours even if you stop using Alfie.
