Alfie at work

You see every change before it counts.

How we work

Start on the basics. Add the next process when you're ready.

You don't need your CRM rules written down to get going. Most teams don't, and that's fine. Alfie starts from sensible standard rules, adjusted to your team.

  1. 01

    Quick start

    We connect HubSpot and Slack and walk you through standard rules for the first process: clean data and required fields. We adjust them to how your team works, and you approve them. Alfie keeps your approved rules and gives you a written copy, your rules of engagement, that you can keep wherever you like, such as Notion or Google Docs. To change a rule, tell us; we update it and send you the new version.

  2. 02

    Checking from day one

    From day one, Alfie checks your HubSpot against your rules and proposes fixes. Changes go live with your approval. Once you trust a rule, you can let it run on its own. Routing and account ownership usually follow in month two.

  3. 03

    Add the next process when you're ready

    When you're ready, we pick the next process together, agree what "done" looks like, build it, and update your rules.

  4. 04

    More complicated processes

    If a process is custom or was never defined, Services takes it on: we work out how it really runs, design the fix with you, document the rules, then Alfie runs it.
