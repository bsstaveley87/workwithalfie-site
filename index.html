You shouldn't still be your company's HubSpot admin.

We built Alfie after watching a fast-growing SaaS team outgrow HubSpot: duplicate accounts, ownership fights, a forecast nobody trusted.

Alfie starts on the basics: clean data and required fields first, then routing and account ownership. You approve a set of standard rules, and from day one Alfie checks your HubSpot against them and proposes fixes. Every change is checked against your rules, and you sign off on what matters.
