Core Alfie

Everyday HubSpot upkeep, run by your rules.

Core Alfie handles the CRM work that keeps coming back. It starts on standard rules you approve, and they grow with your team. Alfie checks your records against them, proposes fixes, and asks a person when the rules say so.

{% include cta.html %}

Five jobs, one set of rules.

  1. 01

    Clean records

    Duplicates found by rule and merged once your admin approves, fields standardized, and deals flagged when they reach a stage without the details your team agreed. The kept value in a merge is chosen by rule, not by guesswork.

  2. 02

    Account ownership

    Assign accounts by territory or segment, on rules you set. Reassignments that break a rule go to the right person for approval.

  3. 03

    Record updates

    Ask in Slack for a change to a contact, company or deal, one record at a time or as a pasted list. Alfie checks it against your rules before it reaches HubSpot.

  4. 04

    Parent and child companies

    Link subsidiaries and regional entities to their parent, using your domain and hierarchy rules. Alfie uses AI to suggest parent and subsidiary company links, and nothing changes in HubSpot until you approve it. Those suggestions go through the same rule checks as every other change Alfie proposes.

  5. 05

    Rules and approvals

    Your rules decide what's allowed, what gets flagged, and who approves, and Alfie applies them to every change it proposes. Alfie keeps your approved rules and gives you a written copy, your rules of engagement, that you can keep wherever you like, such as Notion or Google Docs. To change a rule, tell us; we update it and send you the new version.

Enrichment (adding company or contact data from outside sources) is part of EnrichAI, not core Alfie. The weekly leadership forecast is ForecastAI, also an add-on.

Control

Proposed, checked, approved, done.

Alfie uses AI to understand a request and propose a change. It's designed never to write to HubSpot on that basis alone.

  1. 01

    Proposed

    Alfie reads the request and your HubSpot data and proposes a specific change.

  2. 02

    Checked

    Fixed rule checks, written from your rules, decide whether the change is allowed. If it fails, nothing is written and Alfie says which rule it failed.

  3. 03

    Approved where needed

    If your rules say a person signs off, Alfie asks that person in Slack and waits.

  4. 04

    Verified

    The change is written to HubSpot, then read back by ID. Nothing counts as done until it matches, and a mismatch is flagged, not hidden. You can see and export a record of every change.

How we work

Start on standard rules. Grow into your own.

You don't need a RevOps team or written rules to start. Alfie begins with standard rules for the basics, adjusted to your team and approved by you, so from day one it's checking your HubSpot and proposing fixes. If the founder or a sales, marketing or ops lead runs HubSpot today, Alfie does the upkeep without a full-time hire. If you already have a RevOps person, Alfie works as their second pair of hands: they set the rules, Alfie runs them.

  1. 01

    Quick start

    We connect Alfie to your HubSpot and Slack and walk through standard rules for the first process: clean data and required fields. We adjust them to how your team works: which fields are required, how duplicates are handled, who approves exceptions. You approve them, and they become version one of your rules-of-engagement document.

  2. 02

    Checking from day one

    From day one, Alfie checks your HubSpot against your rules and proposes fixes. Changes go live with your approval. Once you trust a rule, you can let it run on its own.

  3. 03

    Add the next process when you're ready

    When you're ready, we choose the next process with you from a shared list, agree its scope and how we'll judge it done, build it, and review it together. Your rules document updates as each one ships. Typical second process: routing and account ownership.

  4. 04

    Services, when you need them

    If your processes are custom or were never defined, Services takes them on: we work out how each one really runs today, design the fix with you, document the rules, then Alfie runs it.
