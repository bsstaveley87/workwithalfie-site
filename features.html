Core Alfie

The HubSpot upkeep nobody has time for

Every growing team has the same list: duplicates to merge, fields to fix, new contacts with no owner, accounts stranded when someone leaves. It never gets shorter, because the work keeps coming back. Core Alfie takes it on, by rules we set up with you, so your team stops spending evenings on it.

{% include cta.html %}

What core Alfie does

  1. 01

    Merges duplicates

    Alfie finds duplicate contacts and companies using your matching rules, like the two Acme Incs, and drafts each merge with the surviving values chosen by rule. No more side-by-side comparing in two browser tabs. Merges go ahead once your admin OKs them.

  2. 02

    Fills and fixes required fields

    It standardizes messy values (country names, job titles) and catches deals that reach a stage without what your team agreed, like a close date at Proposal. Each owner gets a Slack digest of what's missing on their deals, and your admin gets the exceptions. Pipeline numbers start with the basics: one meaning for each deal stage, close dates and amounts filled in.

  3. 03

    Routes new contacts and companies

    Each new contact and company goes to the right owner by account, territory or segment, and round robin, with your admin approving. No more weekly "who's taking these?" thread.

  4. 04

    Keeps account ownership straight

    Accounts are assigned by territory or segment on your rules. When a rep leaves, Alfie proposes who takes over each account, and nothing moves until your admin approves.

  5. 05

    Tidies properties

    Standardizes emails, phone numbers, websites, countries and states, and clears placeholder values like 'test' or 'n/a'. Alfie proposes each fix and makes it once your admin approves. Lifecycle stage checks can follow once your stage definitions are agreed.

  6. 06

    Links parents and subsidiaries

    AI suggests which companies belong together, like Acme EU under Acme, using your domain and hierarchy rules and the fields already in HubSpot, and you confirm before anything is linked.

  7. 07

    Takes record updates from Slack

    Ask for a change to a contact, company or deal in Slack, as one record or a pasted list, and Alfie makes it once it passes your rules. In the first weeks, your admin OKs each one in Slack. Merges and moving a departing rep's records always need an OK.

Adding outside data, like a contact's phone number or a company's headcount, is EnrichAI, not core Alfie.

You stay in control

Why it's safe to hand this over

AI drafts each change. Fixed checks written from your rules decide whether it's allowed, and Alfie is designed never to write to HubSpot on the AI's word alone. Merges, and anything else your rules reserve for a person, wait for that person in Slack. After each write, Alfie is designed to read the record back by ID and flag any mismatch. You can export a record of each change Alfie makes.

Getting started

You don't need a RevOps team to start

If the founder runs HubSpot today, or a sales, marketing or ops lead who picked it up along the way, Alfie does the upkeep without a full-time hire. If you already have a RevOps person, Alfie does the repetitive part and they set the direction.

  1. 01

    Quick start

    We connect HubSpot and Slack and agree the first rules with you. Which fields are required at each stage? How are duplicates matched? Who gets new contacts?

  2. 02

    First week

    Alfie reads your records and shows you what it would fix, without changing anything. Then it works through the backlog as your admin OKs each change.

  3. 03

    The next process

    When you're ready, we pick it together and Alfie takes it on.

  4. 04

    Services, if you need them

    For processes that are custom or were never defined. About Services
